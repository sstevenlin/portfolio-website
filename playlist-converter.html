Go to your Spotify playlist and copy the song list. You can either:

  • Copy from Spotify web player (song names and artists)
  • Export from Spotify (if you have Spotify Premium)
  • Manually type your favorite songs

✏️ Step 2: Paste Your Songs

Paste your songs in any format (one per line). Examples:

  • Virginia Beach - Drake
